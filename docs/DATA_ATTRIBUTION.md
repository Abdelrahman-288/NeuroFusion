# ISLES 2022 attribution and release record

Pinned release: ISLES 2022 version 2.3.1, Zenodo record 7960856.
Source: https://zenodo.org/records/7960856
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7960856
The release API reports open access and CC-BY-4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Creators: Moritz Roman Hernandez Petzsche, Ezequiel de la Rosa, Roland Wiest,
Uta Hanning, Benedikt Wiestler, and Jan S. Kirschke.

Please retain the release-requested citations in research outputs:

- Hernandez Petzsche, M.R., de la Rosa, E., Hanning, U. et al. ISLES 2022:
  A multi-center magnetic resonance imaging stroke lesion segmentation dataset.
  Scientific Data 9, 762 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01875-5
- de la Rosa, Ezequiel, et al. DeepISLES: a clinically validated ischemic stroke
  segmentation model from the ISLES'22 challenge. Nature Communications 16,
  7357 (2025). This is a citation requested by the dataset release; NeuroFusion
  has not reproduced or inherited that model's clinical validation.

Downloaded source scans remain unmodified. Derived preprocessing artifacts are
identified separately and record orientation/intensity transformations. The
release contains native-space sequences without prior inter-sequence registration.
Archive checksums and the retrieval timestamp are saved after download verification
in `data/downloads/isles-2022-v2.3.1/provenance.json`.
